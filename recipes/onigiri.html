Onigiri (Rice Balls)

A classic bento box food—triangular rice balls with a tasty filling, wrapped in seaweed. Seen in nearly every anime eve

Ingredients

  • 1 cup cooked Japanese rice
  • Salt
  • Filling: tuna mayo, pickled plum, or grilled salmon
  • Nori (seaweed sheets), cut into strips

Steps

  1. Wet your hands and rub a bit of salt on them.
  2. Take a handful of rice and flatten it slightly.
  3. Add a spoonful of filling in the center.
  4. Mold the rice around the filling into a triangle.
  5. Wrap the bottom with a strip of nori.
  6. Serve as-is or pack in a lunchbox!
