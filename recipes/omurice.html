Omurice (Japanese Omelette Rice)

A comforting and cute Japanese dish made with fried rice wrapped in a fluffy omelette, often seen in slice-of-life anime.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup cooked rice
  • 1/4 onion, chopped
  • 1/4 bell pepper, chopped
  • 1/4 cup cooked chicken (or ham), diced
  • 2 eggs
  • 2 tbsp ketchup
  • Salt and pepper
  • Cooking oil
  • Extra ketchup (for decoration)

Steps

  1. Heat oil in a pan and sauté onion and bell pepper.
  2. Add chicken and cook until warm.
  3. Add rice and ketchup; stir-fry and season with salt and pepper.
  4. In another pan, beat and cook the eggs into a thin omelette.
  5. Place the rice in the center of the omelette and fold it over.
  6. Flip onto a plate and decorate with ketchup (hearts or smiley faces are classic!).
