Dango (Sweet Mochi Dumplings)

A chewy Japanese dessert served on skewers, popular in festivals and anime like Clannad.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup glutinous rice flour
  • 1/3 cup warm water
  • Food coloring (optional: pink and green)
  • Bamboo skewers

Steps

  1. Mix rice flour and warm water until a dough forms.
  2. Divide into 3 parts. Add pink and green coloring to two portions; leave one white.
  3. Roll into small balls and boil until they float.
  4. Transfer to cold water, then drain.
  5. Skewer three balls per stick (green, white, pink from bottom to top).
  6. Serve plain or with a sweet soy glaze.
